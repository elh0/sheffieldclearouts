Questions

How quickly can someone come?

Often within a few days, sometimes the same week. Say if you have a deadline, like a tenancy end or a house sale.

Is it really free to ask?

Yes. We're paid by the firm we work with, not by you. You only pay for work you agree to.

What happens to my stuff?

Our partner firm must be a registered waste carrier. Reusable items go to charity or reuse first, and you can ask for a waste transfer note.

Can you help after a bereavement?

Yes. Pick "Probate or bereavement clearance" and tell us anything to keep aside, such as photos, papers or keepsakes.
